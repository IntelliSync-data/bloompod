Getting Started with Bloompod

How should Bloompod be used every day?

You can play Bloompod during your family’s natural daily moments—while your child is playing, resting, or before bedtime.
There is no need for long listening sessions.
Consistency is more important than intensity.

How long should my child listen each day?

Bloompod is designed to blend naturally into everyday life.
Listening for 5–7 minutes consistently each day is more effective than listening for long periods over a short time.
There is no need to create pressure around “enough listening time.”
Simply maintaining familiarity is what matters.

Do parents need to interact while Bloompod is playing?

Interaction is beneficial, but it is not required.
Bloompod supports auditory familiarity in a natural way.
Passive listening within a safe environment is already enough to help establish an initial language foundation.

Caring for Your Bloompod

How should I charge the device?

Use the charging cable provided with the product.
Do not use chargers from unknown or unreliable sources.

Cleaning the device

Use a soft, dry cloth to clean the surface.
Do not use water or strong cleaning agents.

Usage precautions

• Avoid direct contact with water.
• Do not place the device near high heat sources.
• Do not attempt to disassemble the device.

If your Bloompod experiences any technical issues, please contact us for assistance.
